Goal: Reach

The Hook

Clarksville shoppers searching for pasture-raised chicken, eggs, and lamb are finding other farms first. Not because Agape isn't better — because the site isn't built to be found.

  • No meta descriptions — Google writes its own snippets
  • Title tags missing location and product keywords
  • No Google Business Profile — invisible to "near me" searches
  • No structured data — no rich results, no local pack

What's Working

Brand & Mission

Clear regenerative identity, faith-driven story, River Friendly Farm certification. Strong differentiators most farms can't claim.

Content Foundation

Product descriptions with pricing, deposit notes, and pasture-raised details. A real story page, not a template.

  • Squarespace technical base: clean sitemap, correct canonicals, mobile-ready
  • Cumberland River Compact backlink — credible local authority
  • Clarksville Farmers Market presence as an offline channel
01
Weeks 1–2 · Quick Wins

On-Page Fixes

  • Rewrite titles: "Pasture-Raised Meat | Agape Farmstead | Clarksville, TN"
  • Add 150–160 character meta descriptions with location + offer + differentiator
  • One H1 per page; fix the homepage's duplicate headings
  • Alt text on every product image

Highest impact per hour spent. Most of this is done inside Squarespace's editor.

02
Weeks 2–4 · Local Visibility

Get Found Nearby

  • Claim and fully optimize a Google Business Profile — categories, photos, products, hours, reviews
  • Add LocalBusiness schema: name, service area, phone, email, URL
  • Build an ordering FAQ: pickup, delivery radius, deposit process, market days
  • Naturally repeat Adams / Clarksville / Middle Tennessee / within 50 miles

This is where "pasture-raised chicken near me" starts returning Agape instead of the next farm over.

03
Months 2–3 · Content Engine

Seasonal Storytelling

  • 4–6 posts: market days, seasonal availability, certifications, student internships
  • Lean into the differentiator: regenerative + River Friendly + faith-driven
  • Link to the Cumberland River Compact profile — turn a backlink into a partnership
  • Internal links from homepage to key products and contact

Content compounds. Each post is another door into local search.

Why It Matters

Name Collision

Dozens of "Agape Farms" exist nationwide. Branded-plus-location keywords are the only way to own the result.

The Long Tail Wins

"Pasture-raised chicken Clarksville TN" is winnable. "Farm store near me" is not — at least not yet.

Trust Transfers

A shopper who finds Agape via search arrives pre-sold on regenerative values. Less education, faster conversion.
