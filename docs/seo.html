What's Holding It Back

What's Holding It Back

Titles & Snippets

Homepage title is just the brand name. No location, no keywords. Meta descriptions are missing entirely — Google auto-generates poor snippets.

Local Signals

Clarksville appears only in the footer. No street address, map, or service area. No Google Business Profile surfaced — a major gap for near-me searches.

Structure

Multiple H1s on the homepage. Many images lack alt text. No structured data for LocalBusiness, Product, or Organization.

01
Weeks 1–2 · Quick Wins

On-Page Foundations

  • Rewrite titles: brand + location + primary offer (e.g., Pasture-Raised Meat | Agape Farmstead | Clarksville, TN)
  • Write 150–160 character meta descriptions for homepage, products, and story pages
  • One H1 per page; add alt text to every image
  • Add LocalBusiness schema: name, address or service area, phone, email, URL
02
Weeks 2–4 · Local Visibility

Get Found Nearby

  • Claim and fully optimize a Google Business Profile: categories (Farm, Meat Shop), photos, products, hours, website link
  • Encourage reviews that mention location and products
  • Strengthen location mentions naturally: Adams, Clarksville, Middle Tennessee, within 50 miles
  • Add an ordering FAQ section targeting local queries
03
Months 2–3 · Content

Tell the Story

  • Content calendar: 4–6 seasonal posts — market days, certifications, regenerative practices, student internships
  • Lean into the differentiator: regenerative + River Friendly + faith-driven
  • Link to the Cumberland River Compact profile where appropriate
  • Track rankings for long-tails: pasture-raised chicken Clarksville TN, River Friendly Farm Adams
