The Opportunity

The Opportunity

Thanksgiving is the single biggest poultry-buying event of the year. Most families in Middle Tennessee buy frozen birds from big-box stores weeks in advance — or drive to a farm they found by word of mouth.

  • Fresh, never-frozen pasture-raised turkey and chicken is rare in this market
  • Urban Nashville shoppers will pay a premium for provenance they can verify
  • A 200-mile radius captures roughly 2 million people — a market Agape has never touched
  • Delivery removes the biggest barrier: the drive to a rural farm
